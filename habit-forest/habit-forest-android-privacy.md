## The short version

- **Your habits, forest, currency, progress, and settings stay on your device.** We do not have a server, we do not have accounts, and we never receive or store your personal habit data.
- **Analytics are strictly opt-in.** When you first open the app you are asked whether to allow anonymous analytics. If you decline, **no analytics data of any kind is collected or sent** — the analytics system stays switched off. You can change this later in Settings.
- **If you allow analytics**, anonymous usage statistics are collected through **Google Firebase Analytics** and shared with Google. On Android this includes your device's **Advertising ID**.
- **Purchases and subscriptions** are handled entirely by **Apple's App Store** or **Google Play**. We never see your card or payment details.
- **The app shows no ads** and contains no advertising networks.

## 2. What data is collected

### 2.1 Data stored only on your device (never sent to us)

The app stores the following locally on your device (using the operating system's standard local storage). This data **never leaves your device** except through any backup you have enabled in your own Apple or Google account settings. We cannot see it:

- Your habits and habit configuration
- Your forest / garden state and game progress
- In-game currency (seeds), trees generated, achievements, and boosters
- Tutorial progress
- App preferences and settings (including your analytics choice)
- A local record of your purchases and current subscription tier

There is no cloud sync and no user account. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.

### 2.2 Analytics data (only if you opt in)

The **first time you launch the app**, you are shown a consent screen asking whether to enable anonymous analytics. Analytics collection is **disabled by default** and is only turned on if you tap **Accept**. You can turn it on or off at any time in the app's Settings.

If — and only if — you enable analytics, the app uses **Google Firebase Analytics** to collect anonymous, aggregate usage information. This includes:

**Events we log:**
- Total seeds collected (running total)
- Total trees generated (running total)
- Total habits created (running total)
- Tutorial steps completed and tutorial completion

These events contain only counts and a tutorial step number. **We do not log the names, descriptions, or content of your habits.**

**Information Firebase Analytics collects automatically (Google's standard analytics data):**
- An app-instance identifier (a random ID Firebase assigns to your install)
- **Android:** your device's **Advertising ID (AAID)**
- **iOS:** the vendor identifier (IDFV). The app does **not** access the iOS Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt.
- Device model, operating system version, app version, language, and screen information
- Approximate, coarse location (country/region) derived by Google from your IP address; the IP address is not retained by us as an identifier
- Session, engagement, and interaction events, and basic crash/diagnostic signals

This data is processed by Google as described in **Section 4**.

### 2.3 Purchases and subscriptions

The app offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions through **Unity In-App Purchasing**, which routes every transaction to **Apple's App Store** (on iOS) or **Google Play Billing** (on Android).

- Your **payment method, card number, and billing details are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive or store them.**
- The app receives back only the technical result of a purchase — product identifier, store receipt, and entitlement status — which it stores **locally on your device** to unlock what you bought and to restore purchases. This purchase record is not sent to us or any third party.

### 2.4 Store review prompts

The app may ask you to rate it using Apple's built-in review prompt or the **Google Play In-App Review** feature. These prompts are handled on-device by Apple/Google; the app does not collect any personal data through them.

### 2.5 Notifications

The app can send you **local reminders** (for example, habit reminders) scheduled directly on your device. These are **not** push notifications — there is no remote notification server, and no data about you is sent anywhere to deliver them. On Android 13+ and on iOS, the app asks your permission before showing notifications.

### 2.6 Advertising

**The app displays no advertisements and integrates no advertising networks.**

## 3. How we use data

- **On-device data** is used solely to run the app: to save your habits, forest, progress, purchases, and settings.
- **Analytics data** (opt-in only) is used to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used and how to improve it — for example, how many habits people create or where players stop during the tutorial.
- **Purchase data** is used to deliver the items and subscriptions you buy and to restore them if you reinstall.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

## 4. Who your data is shared with

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers required to make the app function:

| Recipient | What they receive | When | Purpose |
|-----------|-------------------|------|---------|
| **Google (Firebase Analytics)** | Anonymous analytics data described in Section 2.2, including the **Android Advertising ID (AAID)** and app-instance ID | Only if you opt in to analytics | Product analytics. ## 5. Data retention

- **On-device data** remains on your device until you delete it (for example, by clearing app data or uninstalling the app). The app includes a reset function that erases all locally stored data.
- **Analytics data** held by Google is retained according to Google's Firebase Analytics retention settings and Google's own policies.
- **Purchase records** are kept locally for as long as the app is installed and are also retained by Apple/Google under their policies.

## 6. Your rights and choices

- **Analytics opt-out:** You can decline analytics on first launch and turn analytics off at any time in the app's Settings. When off, no analytics data is collected.
- **Advertising ID:** On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and limit ad personalization, in your device's Google settings. On iOS the app does not use the advertising identifier.
- **Access and deletion:** Because your habit and game data lives only on your device, you already have full access to it, and you can delete all of it by using the in-app reset option or uninstalling the app. To ask about any data Google or Apple hold on their side, or to exercise rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us at **hbarkine@gmail.com** and we will help direct your request.
- **Notifications:** You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

## 8. Security

Your habit and game data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's own security. Data transmitted to Google Firebase and to the app stores is sent over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we rely on the platform protections provided by Apple, Google, and your device.
